# PolicyDesk — Company Overview and Service Portfolio

## 1. Mission and Consulting Practices
PolicyDesk is a boutique advisory firm providing specialized consulting services to regulated organizations.
Our core consulting practices include:
- **Strategic Advisory**: Executive governance, organizational realignment, and digital operating models.
- **Cloud Architecture & Security**: Cloud infrastructure migration, compliance architecture, and secure workload design.
- **Regulatory Compliance**: Financial services compliance, data privacy framework implementation (GDPR, SOC2), and internal audit readiness.

## 2. Service Tiers
PolicyDesk provides client engagements across three engagement tiers:
1. **Tier 1 - Advisory Retainer**: Continuous weekly advisory sessions, architecture review, and on-demand governance support.
2. **Tier 2 - Project Delivery**: Fixed-scope implementation initiatives with milestone-based deliverable sign-offs.
3. **Tier 3 - Emergency Incident Response**: Rapid response consulting for regulatory inquiry or critical compliance remediation.

## 3. Client Onboarding Process
Every new engagement requires:
1. Execution of the PolicyDesk Standard Master Services Agreement (MSA) and Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement (MNDA).
2. Completion of the Client Risk Questionnaire within 5 business days of contract execution.
3. Assignment of a Lead Engagement Director and dedicated Senior or Principal consultant.
4. An initial scoping workshop and delivery of the signed Statement of Work (SOW).

## 4. Client Confidentiality Rules
All client materials are classified as Restricted Confidential. Consultants must not store client data on personal storage devices or unapproved cloud drives. All project documentation must reside within the client-designated encrypted workspace.
