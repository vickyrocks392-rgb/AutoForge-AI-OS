# PolicyDesk — Standard Operating Procedures (SOP)

## 1. Working Hours and Core Availability
- Standard operating hours are Monday through Friday, 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM Eastern Standard Time (EST).
- Consultants on active client engagements must maintain core availability between 10:00 AM and 4:00 PM EST for client collaboration meetings.

## 2. Timesheet Submission and Billing Cycles
- All billable consultants must log project hours daily in the PolicyDesk Time portal.
- **Weekly Timesheet Deadline**: Weekly timesheets must be submitted no later than **Friday at 6:00 PM EST**.
- Timesheets must be approved by the Engagement Director by Monday at 12:00 PM EST.
- **Invoicing Cycle**: Client invoices are generated bi-weekly on the 1st and 15th of each month, with standard payment terms of **Net 30 days**.

## 3. Communication Guidelines
- **Internal Messaging**: Slack is the designated platform for internal real-time team communication.
- **Client Communications**: Official project deliverable transmissions and contractual communications must be conducted via corporate email (@policydesk.example) with TLS encryption.
- Client Slack or Teams channels may be joined only with prior approval from the Engagement Director.

## 4. Document Retention
Project workpapers, meeting minutes, and draft deliverables must be archived in the project archive vault at project conclusion and retained for seven (7) years in accordance with regulatory recordkeeping standards.
