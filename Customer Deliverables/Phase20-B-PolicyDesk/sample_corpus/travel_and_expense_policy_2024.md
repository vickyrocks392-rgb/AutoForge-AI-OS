# PolicyDesk — Business Travel and Expense Policy (2024 Edition)

> **POLICY STATUS**: ARCHIVED / SUPERSEDED  
> **Effective Dates**: January 1, 2024 through December 31, 2025  
> **Superseded By**: PolicyDesk Business Travel and Expense Policy (2026 Edition, Effective Jan 1, 2026)

## 1. Daily Meal Allowance (Per Diem)
Under this 2024 policy edition, the maximum daily per diem meal allowance for business travel was capped at **$50.00 per day** ($12 breakfast, $15 lunch, $23 dinner). Alcohol expenses are non-reimbursable.

## 2. Personal Vehicle Mileage
When an employee uses their personal vehicle for pre-approved client travel:
- The mileage reimbursement rate is **$0.55 per mile**.
- Tolls and parking fees are reimbursable with valid receipts.

## 3. Lodging and Hotel Limits
Standard business hotel accommodations are capped at **$175.00 per night** excluding local room taxes. Stays exceeding this rate require prior written sign-off from the Practice Director.

## 4. Expense Submission Procedure
- Paper expense claim forms must be printed, accompanied by physical receipts taped to 8.5x11 paper, and submitted monthly to Office Management.
- Receipts are mandatory for all individual items exceeding $25.00.
