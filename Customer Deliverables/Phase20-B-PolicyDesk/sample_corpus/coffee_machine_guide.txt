PolicyDesk Office Kitchenette — Espresso Machine Cleaning & Descaling Guide

1. Daily Maintenance:
   - Empty the grounds drawer and drip tray every afternoon before 5:00 PM.
   - Wipe down the steam wand with a clean damp microfiber cloth immediately after frothing milk.
   - Refill the water reservoir with filtered water from the Brita dispenser only.

2. Weekly Descaling:
   - Run the automated descaling cycle using one packet of Urnex descaling powder every Friday.
   - Fill water tank to the MAX indicator line, dissolve powder, and press and hold the 'Clean' button for 3 seconds.
   - Run two rinse cycles with fresh filtered water before brewing beverages.

3. Coffee Bean Selection:
   - Only dark or medium roast whole beans are permitted in the bean hopper. Flavored or oily beans damage the burr grinder.
