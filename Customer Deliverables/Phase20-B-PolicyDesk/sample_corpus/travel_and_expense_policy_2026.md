# PolicyDesk — Business Travel and Expense Policy (2026 Edition)

> **POLICY STATUS**: ACTIVE / CURRENT  
> **Effective Date**: January 1, 2026  
> **Supersedes**: All prior travel policies, specifically including Travel Policy 2024 Edition.

## 1. Daily Meal Allowance (Per Diem)
Effective January 1, 2026, the daily meal allowance for all business travel has been increased to **$85.00 per day** ($20 breakfast, $25 lunch, $40 dinner). No itemized meal receipts are required when claiming the standard $85 flat per diem on travel days exceeding 10 hours. Alcoholic beverages remain strictly non-reimbursable.

## 2. Personal Vehicle Mileage
When authorized to use a personal vehicle for business travel to client premises:
- The standard reimbursement rate is **$0.67 per mile** (aligned with federal benchmark rate).
- Mileage calculation must use shortest route via Google Maps or Apple Maps printout.
- Routine commuting from home to the PolicyDesk home office is never reimbursable.

## 3. Lodging and Accommodation Cap
Standard hotel lodging is permitted up to **$250.00 per night** (or $320.00 per night in designated high-cost metropolitan areas including New York City, San Francisco, and London).

## 4. Digital Expense Submission and Approval
- All expense reports must be submitted via the PolicyDesk Digital Expense Portal within fifteen (15) days of travel completion.
- Digital itemized receipts are required for all non-per-diem reimbursable expenses exceeding **$15.00**.
- Automated approval routing forwards reports directly to the Engagement Director. Approved reimbursements are disbursed via direct deposit within two (2) payroll cycles.
